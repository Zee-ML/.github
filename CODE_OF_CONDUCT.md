# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **ZeeML** ([https://zeeml.com](https://zeeml.com)) pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy technical and research community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing technical opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code and research feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the overall open-source and academic user base

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via the contact details at [https://zeeml.com/about](https://zeeml.com/about). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
